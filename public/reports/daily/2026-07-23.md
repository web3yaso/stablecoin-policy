Senate Republicans introduced separate legislation on July 23 that would impose new restrictions on rewards offered in connection with stablecoin products. On the state level, Wyoming launched a state-backed stablecoin on the Solana blockchain, and the National Credit Union Administration's stablecoin proposal drew support from credit unions alongside criticism from consumer advocates. Private-sector activity continued in parallel, with Augustus raising $180 million to build clearing-bank infrastructure for the AI and stablecoin sectors and Visa launching a stablecoin treasury engine aimed at institutional finance.
- Europe / UK: The European Central Bank named 36 firms for a digital euro pilot on July 15, while separately warning that stablecoins could drain deposits from traditional banks even with the Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA) framework in place. The EU is planning to revise MiCA in 2027 to cover foreign stablecoin issuers, a move prompted in part by concerns that reserve requirements handed certain issuers a competitive advantage, and by ESMA issuing guidelines that place non-euro stablecoins under tighter scrutiny. Compliance pressure under MiCA is visibly reshaping the market: OKX Europe enabled USDT to USDC conversion to meet MiCA requirements, XRP received MiCA authorization across 30 European countries, and BitPay secured a Dutch MiCA license with plans to expand stablecoin payments across the EU. Circle's euro-denominated stablecoin EURC gained over $110 million in market cap year-to-date, though analysts note that USD stablecoins continue to outpace EUR stablecoins in overall growth. Conflicts between the U.S. Genius Act stablecoin rules and MiCA are meanwhile creating compliance challenges for international firms operating across both jurisdictions.

In Hong Kong, the jurisdiction tested its first licensed stablecoin on the Ethereum blockchain in May 2026, while exchange OSL listed USDKG, a gold-backed stablecoin from Kyrgyzstan, expanding the city's regulated digital asset offerings. Australia recorded its first retail payment in an AUD-denominated stablecoin, settled via Base, marking a practical milestone for domestic stablecoin commerce. Meanwhile, the Reserve Bank of India maintained skepticism, arguing that stablecoins fail the 'singleness, elasticity and integrity' test of money, even as a corporate-led blockchain initiative in Japan advanced plans to issue a yen-denominated stablecoin for B2B payments.
