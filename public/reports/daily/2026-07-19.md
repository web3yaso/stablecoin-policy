## Executive Summary

- U.S. regulators missed the GENIUS Act's one-year deadline for finalizing stablecoin rules, with six agencies failing to meet the statutory requirement and the rulemaking clock effectively reset.
- Delaware enacted a comprehensive banking, money transmission, and stablecoin modernization package, becoming one of the first U.S. states to update its framework in the post-GENIUS Act environment.
- The U.S. government froze nearly $500 million in Tether stablecoins as part of sanctions measures targeting Iran, demonstrating stablecoins' emerging role as a tool of financial statecraft.
- Better Markets raised concerns that the NCUA's stablecoin proposal would expose credit unions to cryptocurrency risks without adequate protective measures, while Paradigm filed a separate comment letter on the same rulemaking.
- Visa launched the Visa Stablecoin Platform using Open USD, targeting 200 million merchants and enabling institutional clients to move onchain without building new infrastructure.
